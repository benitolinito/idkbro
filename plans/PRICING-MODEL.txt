MULTICODE PRICING MODEL

Positioning
-----------

Multiplayer coding is free. Upgrade when your team needs history and control.

MultiCode does not resell AI usage. Hosts connect their existing Codex or
Claude subscription, and guests join without paying.


FREE
----

Price: $0 forever

For developers trying MultiCode, occasional pairing, and ephemeral team
sessions.

Includes:

- One active room per host
- Unlimited guests
- Local VS Code workspace synchronization
- One shared agent session and FIFO prompt queue
- End-to-end encrypted relay
- Isolated agent worktree
- Transactional application of agent changes
- Bring your own Codex or Claude subscription
- Local-only session history
- Community support


PRO
---

Price: $10 per month per host
Annual price: $100 per year per host

For developers and teams that need persistent, governed collaboration without
moving development out of VS Code or paying for every collaborator.

Includes everything in Free, plus:

- Unlimited active rooms
- Persistent encrypted session history
- Session resume
- Context attachments
- Checkpoints and turn-level undo
- Granular diff review
- Participant roles and controls
- Approval policies
- Audit log
- Configurable retention
- Priority relay service
- Priority support


BILLING PRINCIPLES
------------------

- Guests never pay.
- Only users who host Pro rooms require a subscription.
- AI model usage is not included in the subscription price.
- Users bring their existing Codex or Claude subscription.
- No per-guest, per-prompt, or relay-usage pricing at launch.
- Paid value comes from persistence, recovery, governance, and reliability—not
  access to the core multiplayer experience.
